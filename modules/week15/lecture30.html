Lecture 30: Project Presentation & Viva

Communicate your Capstone clearly, defend your methods, and demonstrate professional and ethical conduct. This is the final demonstration of the whole course.

1. Learning Objectives

  • 1 Deliver a 10–15 minute structured presentation of the Capstone.
  • 2 Answer viva questions on methods, quality, DAX/model choices, and ethics.
  • 3 Demonstrate responsible language, limitation awareness, and professional conduct (CLO-5).

2. What You Must Do (Syllabus)

  • Present the business question, data, approach, key insights, and dashboard.
  • Show live interaction (filters, drill-down) for at least one insight.
  • State limitations and ethical considerations explicitly.
  • Answer examiner questions on any stage of the pipeline.

3. Presentation Story Arc (10–15 min)

4. Likely Viva Questions

  • Why did you choose this grain / this primary KPI?
  • How did you handle missing values and outliers? What was the impact?
  • Walk me through one CALCULATE or Time Intelligence measure you wrote.
  • Where could bias have entered your analysis and what did you do about it?
  • What would you change if you had two more weeks?
  • How would you explain this finding to a non-technical manager?

5. Ethics & Professional Conduct (CLO-5)

  • Use precise, non-causal language unless your design supports causality.
  • Never hide data-quality problems; surface them as limitations.
  • Protect personal or sensitive data in every screenshot and file you submit.
  • Credit sources and tools; do not present others’ work as your own.
  • Be honest about uncertainty and about what the dashboard cannot answer.
Examiner mindset
Strong students are not those who claim perfect data — they are those who can explain trade-offs, limitations, and ethical choices clearly.

6. Evaluation Rubric (Guidance)

CriterionWhat strong looks like
Problem framingClear question, relevant KPIs, realistic scope
Data & qualityDocumented cleaning, missingness handled, limitations stated
Analysis & modelCorrect grain, useful measures, sensible DAX / logic
Dashboard & storyCoherent pages, readable visuals, interactive demo
Ethics & communicationHonest limits, careful language, professional delivery
Viva defenceCan explain choices and alternatives under questioning

7. Final Pre-Presentation Checklist

  1. Dashboard opens and filters work offline / on the exam machine.
  2. PDF or image backup of key pages in case of technical failure.
  3. Ethics & Limitations slide or section present.
  4. One sentence for each main insight (what + so-what).
  5. Practised timing (stay inside 10–15 minutes).

8. Summary · End of Course

You have moved from raw data through quality, EDA, visualisation, modelling, DAX, ethics, and stakeholder communication. The Capstone is the proof that you can run the full pipeline responsibly.

Congratulations on completing the Fundamentals of Data Analytics course.
