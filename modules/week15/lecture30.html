Lecture 30: Project Presentation & Viva

Syllabus requirement: explain the pipeline, justify KPIs, and defend insights — with ethics and professional conduct as part of CLO-5.

1. Learning Objectives

  • 1 Present the full analytics pipeline in a clear story arc.
  • 2 Justify each KPI and measure definition under questioning.
  • 3 Defend insights with evidence (not opinions alone).
  • 4 Address data quality limits, bias, and ethical reporting.
  • 5 Close with actionable recommendations the stakeholder can use.

2. What Students Must Do (Syllabus)

  1. Explain pipeline — source → clean → EDA → model → dashboard
  2. Justify KPIs — why these metrics, formulas, and comparisons
  3. Defend insights — charts/tables as evidence; alternative explanations considered

3. Presentation Story Arc (10–15 min)

1. Context

Stakeholder, decision, success criteria.

2. Data & quality

Grain, volume, main cleaning choices.

3. Analysis

2–4 key findings with visuals.

4. Dashboard tour

Live filters answering the decision.

5. Recommendations

Actions, expected impact, next data needs.

6. Limits & ethics

What you cannot claim; privacy/bias notes.

4. Likely Viva Questions

  1. What is the grain of your fact table?
  2. How did you handle missing values and outliers — and why?
  3. Walk through the DAX (or formula) for your main KPI.
  4. Which insight would change if one major assumption failed?
  5. How do your recommendations avoid unfair or privacy-harming use of the data?
  6. What would you do with two more weeks?

5. Ethics & Professional Conduct (CLO-5)

  • Do not overclaim causality from observational correlations
  • Protect personal identifiers; aggregate sensitive groups carefully
  • Report uncertainty and data gaps honestly
  • Avoid charts that mislead through truncated axes or selective filters
  • Credit data sources and document transformations

6. Evaluation Rubric (Guidance)

AreaStrong performance
PipelineClear, complete, reproducible
KPIsDecision-linked, correctly computed
InsightsEvidence-based, prioritized
DashboardUsable, interactive, accurate
VivaConfident, honest about limits
EthicsResponsible reporting demonstrated
